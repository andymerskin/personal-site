## Concept & Objectives

Unify analyzes security data from multiple integrations to generate risk scores and prioritize investigations for organizations of all sizes. The platform provides dashboards, mitigation workflows, training programs, employee scorecards, and recognition features to strengthen security posture.

## Outcomes

- Unify was named the Leader in The Forrester Wave™: HRM Solutions category in 2024, a category that Living Security pioneered as competitors began to emerge.
- Accelerated front-end development and build times using cutting-edge tech (reducing delivery time ~40% vs. traditional stacks).
- Blew customers away with something they had never seen before: a platform that was not only beautiful, but also provided actionable insights and a clear path to improvement by combining integrations, data, and a human-centered design approach tailored to their needs.
- We built alongside our customers, incorporating feedback as the product grew, creating immediate value and a strong foundation for future growth.
