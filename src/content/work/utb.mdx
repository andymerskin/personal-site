I designed the branding and built a landing page for Unite the Bride, a non-profit dedicated to the rehabilitation and reintegration of formerly incarcerated individuals, as well as trafficking survivors. The project focused on communicating their mission to provide critical housing and support services, helping individuals navigate to stable, independent living.

## Goals

- Build a ridiculously simple responsive one-pager to communicate the organization's mission and services.
- Create an easy-to-use donation form, making it easy to specify common amounts donated, with direct credit-card entry.
- Payment backend provided by project partner.
