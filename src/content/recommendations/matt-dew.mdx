Andy and I worked together at Living Security, where I led Engineering and he was an early UI/UX developer on the team. Andy played a key role in bringing the Unify product to life, combining strong technical skills with a genuine curiosity about emerging technologies and how to apply them thoughtfully to real-world software development. **He cares deeply about user experience** and contributed heavily to the design of UI elements and workflows, helping shape a product that was both intuitive and robust.

**Andy will respectfully advocate for what he believes is the right technical or product approach** while remaining open and collaborative. His calm, friendly demeanor made him a pleasure to work with, especially in the fast-paced environment of an early product team. I'd happily work with him again and recommend him without hesitation.
